# Department and Process Discovery Templates

Use these as structured templates for interviews and as field definitions for the application. Do not force every question on every respondent; the adaptive engine should select questions based on scope and existing information.

## E. Interview close-out checklist

- [ ] Respondent role and knowledge boundary recorded.
- [ ] Direct knowledge separated from hearsay/assumption.
- [ ] Unknown and not-applicable answers explicitly recorded.
- [ ] Inputs, outputs, owners and handoffs captured.
- [ ] Decisions, exceptions and escalation paths explored.
- [ ] SLA/TAT definitions clarified rather than only recording a number.
- [ ] Relevant evidence identified or attached.
- [ ] Cross-department dependencies identified.
- [ ] Contradictions and gaps remain visible.
- [ ] Respondent reviewed the summary.
- [ ] Follow-up owners and dates assigned.
