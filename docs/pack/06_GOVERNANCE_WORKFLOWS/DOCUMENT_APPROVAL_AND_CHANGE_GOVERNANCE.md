# Document, Approval and Change Governance

## 1. Purpose

Ensure that policies, SOPs, manuals and work instructions generated by the platform are controlled company documents, not informal AI text. Every document must have a named owner, source basis, status, version and approval route.

## 2. Controlled document types

- Policy.
- Standard Operating Procedure (SOP).
- Process manual / process narrative.
- Work instruction.
- Checklist / job aid.
- Form / template.
- RACI / responsibility matrix.
- Risk and control matrix.
- Training and acknowledgement material.
- Governance standard / operating framework.

Each type may have its own template, required metadata and approval matrix.

## 3. Minimum document metadata

- Document ID/code and title.
- Document type and classification.
- Company/entity, department(s), process(es) and location scope.
- Owner and custodian.
- Author/drafter.
- Version and status.
- Purpose, scope and definitions.
- Effective date, review date and superseded version.
- Reviewers and approvers, with role/scope.
- Approval decisions and timestamps.
- Change summary and reason.
- Source knowledge/process/evidence references.
- Distribution audience and acknowledgement requirement.

## 4. Document generation rules

1. Select an approved template.
2. Select only source records allowed for the document's scope and status.
3. Show the source set and source versions.
4. Generate an editable draft.
5. Separate confirmed content, proposed content, assumptions and gaps.
6. Do not fill missing facts with invented company practice.
7. Flag contradictions, unsupported requirements and stale sources.
8. Run document-specific validation (required sections, owner, definitions, roles, exceptions, records, controls, metrics and review date).
9. Allow responsible owner to edit and submit for review.
10. Store generated model/provider/prompt metadata when AI was used.

## 5. Standard workflow

```text
Draft requested
     |
     v
Source and scope selection
     |
     v
AI-assisted draft + gap report
     |
     v
Document owner review
     |
     v
Functional review (affected departments)
     |
     v
Risk / Legal / Compliance review where applicable
     |
     v
Approval routing per authority matrix
     |
     +---- Changes requested ----> Draft revision
     |
     v
All required approvals complete
     |
     v
Publish immutable version
     |
     v
Notify controlled audience
     |
     v
Acknowledgement + periodic review
     |
     v
Change request / supersession / retirement
```

Not every document requires every reviewer. The document type and impact determine the required route. The route must be configured and versioned.

## 6. Approval model

Support:
- sequential and parallel approval steps;
- required approver by role, named person or delegated authority;
- quorum or all-required approvals;
- conditional route by department, risk, document class or scope;
- delegation with dates and audit history;
- comments, request changes, reject, approve and abstain where allowed;
- resubmission after changes;
- emergency release with named authority, rationale and mandatory retrospective review.

Approval must be enforced by backend/database state transition checks. Hiding an Approve button is not security.

## 7. Versioning

- Draft edits may create draft revisions.
- Submitted review version is frozen for that review round or clearly revision-controlled.
- Approved/published versions are immutable.
- A change after publication creates a new version with change reason and impact assessment.
- Version numbers should follow an agreed format (for example major/minor), not be hardcoded across document types without governance.
- Previous versions remain accessible to authorised users with a clear superseded/withdrawn label.
- Never replace a published file in place.

## 8. Publication and acknowledgement

At publication:
- confirm all required approvals;
- confirm effective date and review date;
- assign immutable version identifier;
- freeze approved content and source references;
- store publication actor/time;
- notify defined audiences;
- capture delivery and acknowledgement separately;
- provide controlled download/view access;
- ensure users do not mistake draft documents for active documents.

Acknowledgement confirms receipt/read state, not necessarily agreement or legal consent. Wording should be explicit.

## 9. Change management

A change request must capture:
- affected document/process/knowledge records;
- reason and evidence;
- requested change;
- urgency and impact;
- affected departments/locations/clients;
- training/system/form/control impacts;
- risk and legal review requirement;
- required approvals;
- implementation owner and due date;
- post-implementation validation.

A published process change should trigger impact analysis on:
- linked SOPs and policies;
- forms, checklists and systems;
- downstream/upstream processes;
- role descriptions and training;
- risk/control mappings;
- SLA and CTQ measures;
- pending interviews/tasks and open disputes.

## 10. Periodic review

- Every controlled document has a review interval or event-based review rule.
- Upcoming and overdue reviews appear on dashboards.
- Owner confirms no change or starts a revision.
- A "no change" review is itself recorded with date, reviewer and reason.
- Events such as new law/regulation, audit finding, customer requirement, incident, system change or organisational restructuring may trigger an early review.

## 11. Required document quality checklist

- Purpose and scope are clear.
- Terms are defined.
- Owner and roles are named by role/function, not just personal names where possible.
- Inputs, outputs and steps are complete.
- Decision points, exceptions and escalations are shown.
- Controls and evidence are described.
- Systems/forms/records are identified.
- TAT/SLA and measurement rules are defined where relevant.
- Related documents are linked.
- Source and assumption report is available.
- Risk/legal review has been completed where required.
- Correct approvals and version metadata are present.
- Publication and communication plan is ready.
